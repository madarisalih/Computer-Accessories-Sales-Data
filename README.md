## 📌 Project Overview

This project is a **Sales Analyst Dashboard** created to analyze sales performance, profit, product performance, regional sales, payment methods, and monthly sales trends.

The dashboard provides an interactive and easy-to-understand view of important business KPIs and helps identify high-performing products, profitable products, and sales trends.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objectives of this dashboard are:

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Track total sales and total profit
- Identify the highest-selling products
- Identify the highest sales month
- Analyze sales by region
- Analyze sales by payment method
- Compare product sales and profit
- Understand profit margins by product
- Monitor total orders and sales quantity

---

## 📌 Key KPIs

The dashboard includes the following KPIs:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| 💰 Total Sales | Overall revenue generated |
| 📈 Total Profit | Total profit generated |
| 📦 Total Sales Quantity | Total quantity of products sold |
| 🏆 Highest Sale Product | Product with the highest sales |
| 📅 Highest Sale Month | Month with the highest sales |
| 🛒 Total Orders | Total number of orders |

---

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Product by Sales
Shows sales generated by each product.

Products analyzed:
- Mouse
- Monitor
- Laptop
- Keyboard
- Headset

### 2. Region by Sales
Displays sales distribution across different regions.

This helps understand which regions contribute to overall sales.

### 3. Product by Profit
Compares the profit generated by different products.

This helps identify products that contribute more to profitability.

### 4. Product Sales Percentage
Shows the percentage contribution of different payment methods such as:

- Card
- Cash
- Net Banking
- UPI

### 5. Monthly Sales Trend
Shows sales performance across different months and helps identify sales patterns.

### 6. Profit Margin by Product
Compares profit margin across products to understand product profitability.
