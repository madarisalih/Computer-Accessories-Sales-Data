# Computer-Accessories-Sales-Data
📊 Sales Analyst Dashboard
This project presents an interactive Sales Analysis Dashboard designed to analyze sales performance across products, regions, months, and payment methods.
📌 Dashboard Overview
The dashboard provides a clear view of:
- 💰 Total Sales
- 💵 Total Profit
- 📦 Total Sales Quantity
- 🏆 Highest Sales Product
- 📅 Highest Sales Month
- 🛒 Total Orders
- 🌍 Region-wise Sales
- 📈 Monthly Sales Trends
- 💳 Sales by Payment Method
- 📊 Product-wise Sales
- 💹 Product-wise Profit
- 📉 Profit Margin by Product
-

📊 Dashboard Visualizations
1. Product by Sales
Shows the sales generated by each product:
- Mouse
- Monitor
- Laptop
- Keyboard
- Headset
2. Region by Sales
Displays the distribution of sales across different regions.
3. Product by Profit
Compares the profit generated by each product.
4. Product by Sales in %
Shows the percentage contribution of different payment methods:
- Card
- Cash
- Net Banking
- UPI
5. Monthly Sales Trend
Shows how sales change across different months.
6. Profit Margin by Product
Compares the profit margin generated by each product.
🎯 Dashboard Filters
The dashboard includes interactive filters for:
- Month
- Product
- Region
These filters allow users to analyze specific segments of the sales data.

🎯 Project Objective
The main objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into an interactive and easy-to-understand business dashboard that helps identify sales trends, profitable products, regional performance, and key business metrics.
📌 Key Insights
- Laptop is the highest-selling product.
- February records the highest monthly sales.
- The dashboard provides a comparison of product-level sales and profitability.
- Payment methods can be analyzed based on their percentage contribution to sales.
- Regional and monthly filters allow more detailed analysis.
